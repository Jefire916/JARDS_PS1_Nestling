How Nest Helper Works

Nest Helper doesn't message everyone. For each family it asks: do we have permission, is there a real reason to message today, and is it safe to say? Only then does it write.

1

Read the data

Past orders, products, and each baby's birth month. No names, photos or addresses.

2

Apply the rules

At most one message: refill first, then size-up, milestone, welcome-back. Only with consent, max one every 7 days.

3

Write the message

A short message for that baby's stage, with the right product and a "Why this message?" button.

4

Check the guardrails

Blocked if it promotes formula, pressures, gives health advice, uses personal data or hides that it's an AI.

5

Hand over to a person

Type HUMAN and a Nestling team member joins the same chat. For complaints, refunds or safety questions, the AI tells you to type HUMAN.

What's real, and what's simulated

Real

  • The decision engine and its rules
  • The Nest Helper chatbot, buttons and quick replies in MantaGO
  • The AI that answers questions, with its safety rules
  • Handover to a real person
  • Every chat button opens the right product page, tagged so you see "You came from Nest Helper"

Simulated

  • The shop, the customers and their orders are invented
  • We run the engine by hand. In the chat, "Meet the families" shows the reminder a family would get, because we aren't connected to a real messaging account
  • Checkout is switched off

Contact us

Open the chat bubble at the bottom right of any page and tap Start Chat.

Ask Nest Helper about sizes, products or orders. To talk to a person, type HUMAN and a Nestling team member will join the same chat (Mon to Fri, 9am to 6pm).

Type STOP at any time to stop reminders.
