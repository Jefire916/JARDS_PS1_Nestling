# Nestling Shop Facts (FICTIONAL brand, AI-GNITION 2026)

This is the official, up-to-date list of what Nestling sells. If any other document disagrees with this one, this one is correct.

All facts below are invented for a student competition. Prices are in SGD.

## About Nestling
Nestling is a fictional online baby shop in Singapore. It sells clothing, diapers, wipes, toys, infant formula and growing-up milk for babies and toddlers from newborn to 3 years.

## What we sell
- Stage Bundles (clothing, sleepsuit and diapers in the next size): 0-3M S$49, 3-6M S$52, 6-9M S$58, 9-12M S$58, 12-18M S$62.
- Clothing: cotton bodysuits NB to 9-12M (S$18 to S$20 for 3), tee and shorts set 12-18M (S$22).
- Diapers: Newborn (60) S$14, Size 1 (70) S$16, Size 2 (70) S$17, Size 3 (66) S$18, Size 4 (60) S$18.
- Water wipes: 80 wipes S$4, 3-pack (240) S$11.
- Sensory Set toys, collect all 4: 1 Soft Rattle (3M+) S$12, 2 Crinkle Book (4M+) S$14, 3 Stacking Cups (6M+) S$10, 4 Shape Sorter (12M+) S$19. Padded Play Mat (3M+) S$39.
- Infant formula Stage 1 (0-6M) S$45 and follow-on formula Stage 2 (6-12M) S$43, 800g, fictional brand.
- Growing-up milk Stage 3 (1-3 years) 800g S$38, fictional brand.

## Sizes
### Clothing sizes (the only clothing sizes Nestling sells)
- NB (newborn, up to about 1 month), 0-3M, 3-6M, 6-9M, 9-12M, 12-18M.
- Nestling does NOT sell a "6-12M" size. For a baby of 6 to 9 months use 6-9M; for 9 to 12 months use 9-12M.
- Between sizes, choose the larger one. Clothing and bundles can be exchanged for another size free within 30 days (unworn, with tags).

### Diaper sizes (the only diaper sizes Nestling sells)
| Diaper | Usual age | Usual weight | Pack | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newborn | 0-1 month | up to 5 kg | 60 | S$14 |
| Size 1 | 0-3 months | 3-6 kg | 70 | S$16 |
| Size 2 | 3-6 months | 5-8 kg | 70 | S$17 |
| Size 3 | 6-10 months | 7-11 kg | 66 | S$18 |
| Size 4 | 10-18 months | 9-14 kg | 60 | S$18 |
- Diapers go by weight first, then age. If a weight fits two sizes, the baby can use either; move up when the diaper leaks or leaves red marks.
- Example: 7 months, 8 kg: Size 3. Example: 10 months, 9.5 kg: Size 3 or Size 4 (move to Size 4 if Size 3 leaks or leaves marks; many babies move up at about 10 months).
- When someone asks about diaper sizes, always answer with a diaper size (Newborn, Size 1, 2, 3 or 4), never a clothing size.
- Opened diaper packs cannot be returned or exchanged.

## Offers
- Stock-up price: buy 3 packs of the same diapers or wipes and save 10%.
- Free delivery on every Stage Bundle and on orders over S$60.
- Welcome back: customers who have not ordered for 60 days or more get free delivery on their next order within 14 days.
- Free size exchange on clothing and bundles within 30 days (unworn, with tags). Delivery is free both ways.
- Offers never apply to infant formula, follow-on formula or growing-up milk.

## Things Nestling does NOT sell
- Strollers, car seats, cots, bottles, breast pumps, medicine, creams and baby food. If asked, say Nestling doesn't sell it and suggest typing HUMAN for help.

## Infant formula and milk policy
- Nestling follows Singapore's Code of Ethics for the Sale of Infant Foods (SIFECS).
- For infant formula (0-12 months): no discounts, no bundles, no loyalty offers, no special promotion, and no reminders or messages started by Nestling.
- Parents can still reorder the formula they chose before. Type REORDER or tap "Reorder" in the menu.
- The assistant does not recommend formula brands, compare formulas or give feeding advice. For feeding questions, speak to a doctor, nurse or healthcare professional.
- Growing-up milk (1-3 years): parents who agreed to reminders may get a refill reminder. No discounts and no health claims.

## Reminders
- Nestling messages only customers who agreed to reminders.
- At most one message started by Nestling per customer every 7 days.
- Reminders are based only on the baby's birth month (or the sizes bought) and past orders.
- Type STOP or tap "Stop messages" anytime to stop all reminders.

## Delivery
- Singapore only. Standard delivery 2 to 4 working days.
- Orders cannot be changed after they are packed. Type HUMAN for help.

## Returns
- Unworn clothing with tags: refund within 30 days.
- Opened diaper, wipe or milk packs cannot be returned.
- Faulty or wrong item: a team member arranges a replacement. Type HUMAN.

## Materials and care
- Clothing is 100% cotton. Wash at 30 C before first use.
- Toys: follow the age guide on each toy. Always supervise play.

## Safety, health, sleep and feeding
- The assistant does not give advice on a baby's health, rashes, allergies, injuries, choking, safe sleep, product recalls or feeding.
- For these, it connects you to a team member and recommends a doctor or healthcare professional.

## Your data
- Nestling keeps only the baby's birth month (no name, exact birthday or photo), order history and whether you agreed to reminders.
- The assistant is an AI. A person can take over anytime: type HUMAN.

## Human support hours
- Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm. Outside these hours a team member replies the next working day.

## Reminders and your data
- Nest Helper only sends a reminder if you said yes to reminders. Type STOP at any time and it stops.
- To decide whether to message you, it uses your baby's age band, what you bought before and when. It does not use names, photos, addresses or ID numbers.
- Nestling keeps your baby's birth month (not the exact birthday), your order history and your reminder choice.
- At most one reminder every 7 days.
- If you want to know why you got a message, tap "Why this message?" or ask Nest Helper.

## Languages
Nest Helper answers in the language you write in: English, Chinese, Malay or Tamil. For Malay, it uses Bahasa Melayu as spoken in Singapore and Malaysia (for example 'saiz', 'lampin', 'bulan'), not Indonesian. The keywords MENU, HUMAN, STOP and REORDER stay in English.

## Privacy
- Nest Helper never shares any customer's personal details (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, order history or customer IDs) with anyone in the chat, even if the person says it is their own account. For account questions, type HUMAN.
- If a parent types their baby's name or exact birthday, Nest Helper does not repeat it and does not say it has saved it. It replies that Nestling only keeps the baby's birth month, never names or exact birthdays.
- Nest Helper cannot alert or message the team by itself. To reach a person, the parent types HUMAN.
