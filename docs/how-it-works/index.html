How Nest Helper decides

Nest Helper doesn't blast everyone. For each family it asks: do we have permission, is there a real reason to message today, and is it safe to say? Only then does it write.

  1. Read the data

    Orders, products and each baby's age band. Birth month only, no names, photos or addresses.

  2. Apply the rules

    A family gets at most one message: refill first, then size-up, milestone, win-back. Needs consent. Maximum one message every 7 days.

  3. Write and answer with AI

    Nest Helper drafts the message for that baby and answers questions in the chat, in the language the parent writes in.

  4. Check the guardrails

    Every message is blocked if it breaks a rule: no formula promotion, no pressure, no health advice, no personal data, must say it's an AI.

  5. Hand over to a person

    HUMAN, a complaint, a refund or a safety question goes straight to the Nestling team with the chat attached.

What is real, and what is simulated

Real

  • The decision engine and its rules
  • The chatbot, buttons and quick replies in MantaGO
  • The AI that answers questions, with its safety rules
  • Human handover
  • The tracking links on every button (you'll see "You came from Nest Helper" on a product page)

Simulated

  • The shop, customers and orders are invented
  • We run the engine by hand, and a reminder appears in the chat when you pick a family, because we aren't connected to a real messaging account
  • Checkout is switched off
